FULL SYSTEM ACCESS

I bought Full System Access, but the game is still limited.
Open the Full System Access screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase.

Does the purchase work on my other devices?
Yes, on devices of the same platform signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. Use Restore Purchase on the new device. Purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android.

Refunds
Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: Apple · Google Play.

PROGRESS

Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. The game works fully offline. There is no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Your Full System Access purchase is kept by Apple or Google and can always be restored.

Can I start over?
Yes: System Config → Reset All Progress. This deletes all local progress and cannot be undone.

ACHIEVEMENTS & LEADERBOARDS

Achievements and leaderboards use Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android. If they do not appear, check that you are signed in to the platform service on your device.
