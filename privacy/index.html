PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the SORDEX mobile game (“the Game”) for iOS and Android handles information. The Game is developed and published by Filipp Panteleev (Hyrax Project), referred to as “we” below.

In short: the Game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. We do not ask for your name, email or location. The only data that leaves your device is what the app stores and the payment service need to handle the optional in-app purchase and platform achievements.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Game progress, records, achievements progress and settings (language, sound, display) are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you delete the app or use System Config → Reset All Progress.

IN-APP PURCHASE

The Game offers one optional one-time purchase, Full System Access. Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details.

To check whether you own the purchase and to restore it, the Game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history and receipt for this app, and basic technical data (such as the app version, OS version, country/currency of the store and IP address) needed to deliver the service. This ID is not linked to your name, email or account identity, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

ACHIEVEMENTS AND LEADERBOARDS

If you are signed in, the Game reports achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android). These services are operated by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies: Apple · Google.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers (IDFA / Advertising ID).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

CHILDREN

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

YOUR RIGHTS AND DATA DELETION

Local data can be deleted at any time by deleting the app or using System Config → Reset All Progress. Because the purchase ID used by RevenueCat is anonymous, we cannot link it to you on our own; if you want purchase records associated with your device deleted, or have any other privacy request (including under GDPR), contact us at the address below and we will help. Deleting purchase records does not remove your purchase from Apple or Google, but you may need to restore it again.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
